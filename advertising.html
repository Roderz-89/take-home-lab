Advertising

Updated 17 October 2026 · Tax year 2026/27

Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs. The site may show advertising served by Google AdSense on a small set of pages. Ads pay for the site; they do not change the calculator or the figures in any guide.

Advertising stays off until you accept

  • On your first visit to any page, a consent dialog headed “Take-Home Lab asks for your consent to use your personal data” asks first. It lists two purposes: store and/or access information on a device, and personalised advertising and ad measurement (Google). The AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested until you consent.
  • Consent loads the Google AdSense script and requests ads on the pages listed below. Google may then use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise and measure ads; see Cookies.
  • Manage options lets you choose: Strictly necessary is always on, and the switch for personalised advertising and ad measurement is off by default. Save choices with the switch on loads ads; with it off, nothing ad-related loads.
  • Do not consent keeps advertising off: the ad slot stays hidden and Google’s script is never loaded.
  • You don’t need to consent to use the calculator. Your choice applies to this site only and is saved in this browser’s localStorage under thl-consent, as a small record of the notice version (v: 2), your choice (essential or all) and the time you chose. It is not a cookie and is not sent to us. Choices made with the earlier banner are not carried over, so everyone is asked once more. See Cookies for the full format.

Where ads can appear

  • The home page, only after you consent.
  • Three guides: pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK, again only after you consent.
  • No other page loads AdSense, even after you accept. That includes the salary guides, the guides index, glossary, about, methodology, contact, and the privacy, cookies, disclaimer and advertising pages.

Changing your choice

Use the Privacy preferences link at the bottom of any page to reopen the dialog and choose again. If you switch to Do not consent on a page where ads have already loaded, the page reloads so the ads stop. You can also clear site data for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser, and manage ad personalisation in Google’s ad settings.

Your calculator inputs

We do not sell your calculator inputs. Salary figures you type into the calculator stay in your browser for the calculation and are not passed to Google.

ads.txt

The root ads.txt file authorises Google (google.com) as the direct seller of advertising on Take-Home Lab, for the Rodway Labs AdSense account.
