Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs. This site may display advertising served by Google (Google AdSense) on selected pages. Consent for advertising on those pages is collected by Google’s consent message.

What that means

  • Ads, if shown, are provided by Google. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to your choices and applicable law.
  • We do not sell your calculator inputs. Salary figures typed into the take-home calculator stay in your browser for the calculation.
  • AdSense is wired only on the home page and three guides: pay rise, salary sacrifice, and Scotland vs rest of UK. Salary landings and other guides do not load AdSense.
  • Ad units are not placed on legal stub pages (privacy, cookies, disclaimer, contact, advertising), the guides index, glossary, about, methodology, or other guides outside that small set.
  • On those four pages, Google’s consent message (from a Google-certified consent management platform, delivered through the AdSense tag) asks for your advertising choice: Consent, Do not consent or Manage options. Take-Home Lab does not show a separate ad-consent banner.
  • To change your choice later, use the “Privacy and cookie settings” link if Google shows one, or clear site data for takehomelab.co.uk and reload an ad page.
