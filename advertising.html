Advertising

Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs. The site may show advertising served by Google AdSense on a small set of pages. Ads pay for the site; they do not change the calculator or the figures in any guide.

Advertising stays off until you accept

  • On every page that can show ads, a banner offers Essential only and Accept ads. The AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested until you press Accept ads.
  • Essential only keeps advertising off: the ad slot stays hidden and Google’s script is never loaded.
  • Accept ads loads the AdSense script for publisher ca-pub-7612291779397704 and requests ads. Google may then use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads; see Cookies.
  • Your choice is saved in this browser’s localStorage under thl-consent (essential or all). It is not a cookie and is not sent to us.

Where ads can appear

  • The home page, behind the consent banner.
  • Three guides: pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK, behind the same consent banner. The AdSense script loads only after you press Accept ads.
  • No other page loads AdSense. That includes the salary guides, the guides index, glossary, about, methodology, contact, and the privacy, cookies, disclaimer and advertising pages.

Changing your choice

Use Reset my cookie choice on the Cookies page, or clear site data for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser. The banner will appear again on pages that can show ads. You can manage ad personalisation in Google’s ad settings.

Your calculator inputs

We do not sell your calculator inputs. Salary figures you type into the calculator stay in your browser for the calculation and are not passed to Google.

ads.txt and publisher ID

The root ads.txt file authorises Google (google.com) to sell inventory for publisher pub-7612291779397704 (DIRECT). In page code the same account appears as ca-pub-7612291779397704.
