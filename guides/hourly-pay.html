GUIDE

From £15 an hour to a packet

Last checked 30 September 2026 · Tax year 2026/27 · England, Wales or Northern Ireland · No pension · No student loan

An hourly rate is not a salary until the hours are stated. This page uses 37.5 hours a week and 52 paid weeks. A contract with fewer hours, unpaid weeks or overtime will not match.

Worked example

  • £15 × 37.5 × 52 = £29,250.00 of gross in this model
  • Income tax: £3,336.00
  • Employee National Insurance: £1,334.40
  • Illustrated take-home: £24,579.60 a year (£2,048.30 a month)

Run £29,250.00 in the calculator

What this page does not decide

It does not convert a rota. Holiday pay, overtime premia and a tax code are outside the illustration.

Sources: HMRC rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027. National Insurance is modelled on the year, not each pay period.
