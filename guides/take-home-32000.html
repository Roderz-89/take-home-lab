Take-home pay on £32,000

By the Rodway Labs editorial team · Checked 12 November 2026 · Editorial policy

Updated 12 November 2026 · Tax year 2026/27 · England, Wales or Northern Ireland · No pension · No student loan · Standard personal allowance

Scope: England, Wales and Northern Ireland only, one salary. For Scotland see Scotland versus the rest of the UK; for loan detail see the student loan guides.

On a £32,000 salary you take home £26,559.60 a year — £2,213.30 a month or £510.76 a week — in England, Wales or Northern Ireland for 2026/27, with no pension and no student loan. That is after £3,886.00 income tax and £1,554.40 National Insurance. Every undergraduate plan except Plan 4 now takes something, so £32,000 is where the plans differ most.

Worked example — £32,000.00 gross

A salary of £32,000.00 with nothing else in the model: standard personal allowance of £12,570, no workplace pension, no student loan.

  • Gross pay: £32,000.00
  • Personal allowance in this run: £12,570.00
  • Taxable pay: £32,000.00 − £12,570.00 = £19,430.00
  • Income tax: £3,886.00
  • Employee National Insurance: £1,554.40

You keep £26,559.60 of £32,000.00 in this illustration. That is not a budget until a payslip agrees.

Moving north of the border?

On Scottish income tax bands, £32,000 leaves £26,574.53 a year (£2,214.54 a month): £14.93 more than here. NI does not change. Compare with the nearest Scottish worked example, £30,000 in Scotland, or Scotland versus the rest of the UK.

The £32k angle: which student loan bites

£32,000 is above the Plan 2 threshold (£29,385) and the Plan 5 threshold (£25,000) but below Plan 4 (£33,795). Plan 2 takes £235.35 a year (£19.61 a month), Plan 5 takes £630.00 (£52.50 a month), and a Scottish Plan 4 borrower repays nothing. Compare all plans on Plan 2.

Comparing loan plans at £32,000

  • Plan 1: £459 a year.
  • Plan 2: £235.35.
  • Plan 4: nothing (threshold £33,795).
  • Plan 5: £630.
  • Postgraduate Loan: £660, on top of any undergraduate plan.

Two graduates on the same £32,000 can therefore see monthly take-home differ by over £50. If you hold Plan 2 and a Postgraduate Loan together, both are taken from the same pay against their own thresholds: £895.35 a year.

What is £32,000 a month after tax? £2,213.30 in England, Wales or Northern Ireland with no pension or loan, and £2,214.54 in Scotland.
