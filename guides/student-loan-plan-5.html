Plan 5 on £35,000

Last checked 30 September 2026 · Tax year 2026/27 · England, Wales or Northern Ireland · No pension · One loan plan

The calculator models one student loan plan at a time. A real payslip can show more than one. The threshold and rate below are the 2026/27 HMRC collection figures used on this site.

Plan 5 applies to new undergraduate loans issued from 6 April 2026 in England. This page uses the 2026/27 collection threshold of £25,000.

Worked example

Salary £35,000.00. Plan 5 threshold £25,000. Rate 9% of earnings above the threshold.

  • Income tax: £4,486.00
  • Employee National Insurance: £1,794.40
  • Plan 5: 9% × (£35,000 − £25,000) = £900.00
  • Illustrated take-home: £27,819.60 a year (£2,318.30 a month)

Without the loan, the same salary illustrates as £28,719.60. The loan is the difference in this model.

Run this salary in the calculator

What this page does not decide

It does not say which plan a person is on. The Student Loans Company and the payslip do. It does not model two plans together.

Sources: HMRC rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027. National Insurance is modelled on the year, not each pay period.
