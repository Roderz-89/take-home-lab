What a £5,000 bonus does to take-home

Last checked 30 September 2026 · Tax year 2026/27 · England, Wales or Northern Ireland · No pension · No student loan

A bonus is still pay. In this annual model a £5,000 bonus on £35,000 is the same stack as a £40,000 salary. Payroll may not treat the bonus month that way: National Insurance is worked out each pay period, and a one-off bonus can take a different NI slice in the month it is paid.

Worked example — annual model

  • £35,000 salary, no bonus: take-home £28,719.60
  • £35,000 plus a £5,000 bonus, treated as £40,000 for the year: tax £5,486.00, NI £2,194.40, take-home £32,319.60
  • Illustrated extra in the packet: £3,600.00 of the £5,000

Both figures sit in the basic-rate band and below the NI upper earnings limit, so the marginal rate in this annual model is 28%.

What this page does not decide

It does not predict the month the bonus lands. Check that payslip before treating the annual figure as the cash that month.

Sources: HMRC rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027. National Insurance is modelled on the year, not each pay period.
