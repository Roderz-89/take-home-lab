Cookies

The take-home calculator does not need an account and does not set its own advertising cookies.

If the host platform (Cloudflare) sets a strictly necessary cookie to deliver or protect the page, that is a hosting function, not an ad tracker.

Google advertising

When ads via Google AdSense are enabled, Google may set or read cookies (and similar technologies) to serve and measure advertisements. Those cookies are controlled by Google. You can manage ad personalisation through Google’s ad settings and, where required, through a consent choice on this site once a live consent banner is added.

Live AdSense JavaScript is not embedded on this build yet. When it goes live, this page will continue to name Google and link to Advertising and Privacy. Non-essential advertising cookies should only be used with an appropriate consent mechanism where the law requires it.
