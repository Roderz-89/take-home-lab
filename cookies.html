Cookies

The take-home calculator does not need an account and does not set its own advertising cookies.

If the host platform (Cloudflare) sets a strictly necessary cookie to deliver or protect the page, that is a hosting function, not an ad tracker.

Your consent choice

On pages that may show ads, Take-Home Lab shows a consent banner before any Google advertising cookies load. You can Accept (allow advertising cookies via Google AdSense) or Reject / essential only (no AdSense script, no ad units). The choice is stored in your browser as rl-ads-consent in localStorage so we remember it on later visits to those pages. Clearing site data resets the banner.

Google advertising

When you accept, we load Google AdSense JavaScript. Google may set or read cookies (and similar technologies) to serve and measure advertisements. Those cookies are controlled by Google. You can also manage ad personalisation through Google’s ad settings.

AdSense is only wired on selected content pages (home and a small set of guides), not on legal stubs (privacy, cookies, disclaimer, contact, advertising) or the guides index. See Advertising and Privacy.

How to change your mind

Clear this site’s data in your browser (or remove the rl-ads-consent localStorage key) and reload an ad-capable page. The consent banner will appear again. Rejecting later does not unload ads already loaded in that tab until you refresh.
