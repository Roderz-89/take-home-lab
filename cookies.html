Cookies

The take-home calculator does not need an account and does not set its own advertising cookies.

If the host platform (Cloudflare) sets a strictly necessary cookie to deliver or protect the page, that is a hosting function, not an ad tracker.

Your consent choice

On the pages that can show ads, advertising consent is collected by Google’s consent message, which comes from a Google-certified consent management platform and is delivered through the Google AdSense tag. Take-Home Lab does not run its own ad-consent banner. Google’s message gives you three options: Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options to choose individual purposes and vendors. Google stores your choice and applies it to the ads it serves on this site.

Google advertising

On the pages listed below, the page loads Google AdSense JavaScript. Google may set or read cookies (and similar technologies) to serve and measure advertisements, in line with the choice you make in Google’s consent message. Those cookies are controlled by Google. You can also manage ad personalisation through Google’s ad settings.

AdSense is only wired on the home page and three guides (pay rise, salary sacrifice, and Scotland vs rest of UK), not on legal stubs (privacy, cookies, disclaimer, contact, advertising) or the guides index. See Advertising and Privacy.

How to change your mind

If Google shows a “Privacy and cookie settings” link on an ad page, use it to reopen the consent message and change your choice. Otherwise, clear this site’s data (cookies and site storage for takehomelab.co.uk) in your browser and reload one of the ad pages; Google’s message will ask again.
