Cookies

Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs. The take-home calculator needs no account, and Take-Home Lab does not set its own advertising cookies. If Cloudflare, our host, sets a strictly necessary cookie to deliver or protect a page, that is a hosting function, not an ad tracker.

The consent banner

On the home page, a banner at the bottom of the screen says: “Advertising stays off until you accept. Essential pages work either way.” It has two buttons:

  • Essential only saves your choice, hides the banner and keeps the advertising slot hidden. The Google AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested.
  • Accept ads saves your choice, hides the banner, then loads the Google AdSense script (publisher ca-pub-7612291779397704) and requests ads for the page.

Until you press one of the buttons, the banner stays on screen and no AdSense script is loaded. The calculator works the same whichever you choose.

What is stored

Your choice is stored only in your browser’s localStorage on this device, under the key thl-consent. It is not a cookie and is not sent to us. It has one of two values:

  • essential when you pressed Essential only
  • all when you pressed Accept ads

If nothing is stored, the banner is shown again on your next visit to the home page.

Cookies Google may set after you accept

Only after Accept ads does the page load Google AdSense. Google may then set or read cookies and similar technologies to serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. These can include first-party cookies on takehomelab.co.uk such as __gads, __gpi and __eoi, and Google cookies such as IDE and test_cookie on doubleclick.net. Google controls these cookies and their lifetimes; see Google’s advertising technologies page. You can also manage ad personalisation in Google’s ad settings.

Which pages can show ads

The consent banner and its ad slot are on the home page. Three guides also carry an ad slot: pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK. Those three guides do not yet show the banner or read your thl-consent choice: they currently load the Google AdSense tag with the page, and Google’s own consent tools apply there. No other page loads AdSense, including the calculator salary guides, the guides index, glossary, about, methodology, contact and the legal pages.

How to change your choice

The banner is hidden once you have chosen. To choose again, press the button below. It deletes the thl-consent entry from this browser, and the banner will appear the next time you open the home page.

You can also clear site data (cookies and site storage) for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser settings. Resetting your choice does not delete cookies Google has already set; clear those in your browser or through Google’s ad settings.
