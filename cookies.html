Cookie policy

Updated 17 October 2026 · Tax year 2026/27

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as browser storage, work in much the same way. This policy explains what Take-Home Lab, published by Rodway Labs, uses and why.

How consent is collected

On your first visit to any page, our own consent pop-up, run by Rodway Labs, asks for your choice. It is headed “Take-Home Lab asks for your consent to use your personal data to:” and lists the only two purposes we ask consent for:

  • personalised advertising and content, advertising and content measurement, audience research and services development (Google AdSense, our only advertising partner); and
  • store and/or access information on a device.

It explains that your personal data will be processed and information from your device (cookies, unique identifiers and other device data) may be stored by, accessed by and shared with Google, our advertising partner, or used specifically by this site. It also says that Google may process some data on the basis of legitimate interest, which you can object to under Manage options: choosing Do not consent, or leaving Advertising off, means the Google script never loads, so Google receives nothing from this site for advertising on either basis.

  • Consent saves your choice, closes the pop-up and loads the Google AdSense script, on pages that carry an ad slot only.
  • Do not consent saves your choice and closes the pop-up. The ad slot stays hidden; no Google advertising script loads and no advertising cookies are set.
  • Manage options opens a second screen with Strictly necessary (always on) and a switch for Advertising (Google AdSense), which is off unless you have already consented. From there you can choose Do not consent, Save choices (saves the switch as set) or Consent, or go Back.

The pop-up stays until you choose; the Esc key does not close it. Until you choose, and if you choose Do not consent, no Google advertising script loads anywhere on the site and no advertising cookies are set. No non-personalised or “limited ads” request is made instead: without consent there are simply no ads. You do not have to consent to use the calculator or read the guides.

Strictly necessary

These are always on and are not part of the consent choice.

  • __cf_bm and cf_clearance (Cloudflare cookies, typically about 30 minutes): set when Cloudflare’s bot and security protection runs, to help keep the site available and secure.
  • thl-consent (browser local storage, not a cookie): remembers your consent choice (whether you allowed advertising, and when) so the pop-up does not ask on every page. It is a small record such as {"v":3,"ads":false,"ts":"2026-10-17T07:00:00.000Z"}: v is the format version, ads is true if you allowed advertising and false if not, and ts is when you chose. It is not sent to us and stays until you change your choice or clear this site’s browser storage. A choice saved in the earlier format (version 2, with "choice":"all" or "essential") is converted automatically, keeping your decision and its date; anything older is ignored and the pop-up asks again.

Calculator

The calculator runs in your browser. The figures you enter are not saved in cookies or browser storage and are not sent to us; reloading or closing the page clears them. You do not have to accept any cookies to use it.

Analytics

This site runs no analytics script and sets no analytics cookies.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Our advertising partner is Google, through Google AdSense. The AdSense tag loads only on pages that carry an ad slot: the home page and three guides, pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK. Every other page, including the salary guides, the guides index, glossary, methodology, about, contact and the legal and policy pages, never loads it, even after you accept. Until you choose, and if you choose Do not consent, no Google advertising script loads and no advertising cookies are set.

If you accept, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to show ads (which may be personalised) and measure how they perform, for example __gads, __gpi and __eoi on takehomelab.co.uk and IDE and test_cookie on doubleclick.net. Their names and lifetimes are set by Google and vary; see how Google uses cookies and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Changing your choice

Use the “Privacy preferences” link at the bottom of every page to reopen the pop-up and change or withdraw your choice. If you switch from consenting to Do not consent, the page reloads so the Google script is removed. You can also clear this site’s browser storage and cookies, after which the pop-up will ask again, or block cookies altogether. Blocking cookies may affect ads but not the calculator. Changing your choice does not delete cookies Google has already set; clear those in your browser. You can manage personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com.

See also our privacy notice and advertising disclosure.
