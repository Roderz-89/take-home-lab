Cookies

Updated 14 October 2026 · Tax year 2026/27

Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs. The take-home calculator needs no account, and Take-Home Lab does not set its own advertising cookies. If Cloudflare, our host, sets a strictly necessary cookie to deliver or protect a page, that is a hosting function, not an ad tracker.

The “We value your privacy” dialog

On every page of Take-Home Lab, a dialog headed “We value your privacy” opens the first time you visit, if you have not chosen yet. It says: “Rodway Labs and our advertising partners, including Google AdSense, ask you to consent to cookies and device storage for personalised advertising and ad measurement.” You don’t need to consent to use the calculator, and your choices apply to this site only.

You can expand the two purposes the dialog asks about. These are the only purposes used on Take-Home Lab; the site runs no analytics.

  • Store and/or access information on a device: cookies or similar data stored on or read from your device. Take-Home Lab itself stores only your choice (see below).
  • Personalised advertising and ad measurement (Google): Google AdSense may use cookies and browsing information to choose relevant ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

The dialog has these buttons:

  • Accept All saves your choice, closes the dialog, then loads the Google AdSense script and requests ads, on pages that carry an ad slot.
  • Options shows your choices: Essential (always on; it only remembers your choice) and a switch for Personalised advertising and measurement (Google), which is off by default. From there:
    • Save choices saves what you set. With advertising switched on it works like Accept All; with it off it works like Essential only.
    • Essential only saves your choice, closes the dialog and keeps the ad slot hidden. The Google AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested.

Until you choose, no AdSense script is loaded and no ads are requested. Closing the dialog with the Esc key does not count as consent: nothing is saved, advertising stays off, and the dialog asks again on the next page you open. The calculator works the same whichever you choose.

What is stored

Your choice is stored only in your browser’s localStorage on this device, under the key thl-consent. It is not a cookie and is not sent to us. It is a small record with three fields: v (the version of this notice, currently 2), choice and ts (when you chose). For example: {"v":2,"choice":"essential","ts":1760140800000}. choice is one of:

  • essential when you chose Essential only, or saved your choices with advertising off
  • all when you chose Accept All, or saved your choices with advertising on

Choices made with the earlier Take-Home Lab banner (stored as a plain essential or all) are not carried over: the new dialog asks everyone once more. If no version 2 choice is stored, the dialog opens on the next page you visit.

Cookies Google may set after you accept

Only after Accept All (or Save choices with advertising on) does a page load Google AdSense. Google may then set or read cookies and similar technologies to serve, personalise, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. These can include first-party cookies on takehomelab.co.uk such as __gads, __gpi and __eoi, and Google cookies such as IDE and test_cookie on doubleclick.net. Google controls these cookies and their lifetimes; see Google’s advertising technologies page. You can also manage ad personalisation in Google’s ad settings.

Which pages can show ads

Four pages carry an ad slot: the home page and three guides, pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK. Each one waits for your consent in the dialog before loading the Google AdSense script; until then the slot stays hidden and no ads are requested. Your thl-consent choice applies on all four. No other page loads AdSense, even after you accept, including the calculator salary guides, the guides index, glossary, about, methodology, contact and the legal pages.

How to change your choice

Every page has a Privacy preferences link at the bottom. It reopens the dialog so you can choose again; your current choice is shown under Options. If you switch from Accept All to Essential only on a page where ads have already loaded, the page reloads so the ads stop.

You can also clear site data (cookies and site storage) for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser settings. Changing your choice does not delete cookies Google has already set; clear those in your browser or through Google’s ad settings.
