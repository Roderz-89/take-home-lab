UK take-home pay — 2026/27

Personal allowance £12,570; tapered by £1 for every £2 of income above £100,000. Employee NI 8% then 2%. Last checked September 2026.

Net pay reduces taxable pay, not NI. Salary sacrifice reduces both in this model. Your scheme rules can differ.

Plan 5 applies to new undergraduate loans issued from 6 April 2026 in England. You can hold more than one plan in real life; this tool models one at a time.

Income tax slices

    Method: annual PAYE-style stack. Income tax on pay after personal allowance and any modelled pension relief. Employee Class 1 NI at 8% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above, modelled on the year rather than each pay period. Student loan as a percentage of earnings above the plan threshold.

    Illustrative only — not a tax calculation you can file, not personalised advice, not an offer of credit. Blindness, marriage allowance, tax codes other than a standard 1257L, the High Income Child Benefit Charge, savings and dividends are omitted.

    EXAMPLE WORKED CASE — £35,000 IN ENGLAND

    £35,000 · no pension · no student loan · rest of UK

    Taxable pay after the £12,570 allowance is £22,430. Basic-rate tax is 20% of that (£4,486). Employee NI on the slice between £12,570 and £35,000 is 8% (£1,794.40). Illustrated take-home is £28,719.60 a year, £2,393.30 a month.
