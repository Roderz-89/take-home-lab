METHOD

How the calculator stacks a packet

Last checked 30 September 2026 · Tax year 2026/27

Take-Home Lab runs an annual PAYE-style stack in the browser. Nothing you type is sent to Rodway Labs. The result is an illustration with the assumptions below, not a figure you can file.

Income tax

England, Wales and Northern Ireland use the main rates: 20% on taxable pay up to £37,700, 40% to £125,140, 45% above. The standard personal allowance is £12,570, reduced by £1 for every £2 of income above £100,000. Scotland uses the 2026/27 Scottish bands on non-savings, non-dividend income. The model assumes a 1257L-style allowance unless the taper applies.

National Insurance

Employee Class 1 is modelled on the year: 8% between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2%. Real payroll calculates National Insurance each pay period. A bonus month can therefore differ from this page.

Student loans

One plan at a time. Plan 1, 2, 4 and 5 at 9% above the 2026/27 HMRC threshold. Postgraduate loan at 6% above £21,000. Plan 5 is modelled as the plan for new undergraduate loans issued from 6 April 2026 in England, threshold £25,000.

Pension

A percentage of gross. Net pay reduces taxable pay, not NI, in this model. Salary sacrifice reduces both. Relief at source is not modelled. The employer booklet decides which design a scheme actually is.

Left out

Other tax codes, marriage allowance, blind person’s allowance, the High Income Child Benefit Charge, savings and dividends, company benefits, court orders, payroll giving, and employer National Insurance.

Sources: HMRC rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027; Scottish Government Scottish Income Tax 2026 to 2027.
