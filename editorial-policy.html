Editorial policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Take-Home Lab is published by Rodway Labs, an independent UK publisher. No employer, payroll provider, bank or adviser pays for or approves our content.

Who writes and checks

Guides are written and edited by the Rodway Labs editorial team. We do not publish individual author names. Every £ figure is produced by the same js/tax.js engine that powers the calculator, then checked by hand, band by band, before publication. A guide is not published until its figures match the live calculator.

Sources

We use primary sources only for rates and thresholds: HMRC, GOV.UK and the Scottish Government. Each guide names its sources and the date they were last checked. The full list is on the methodology page.

Review cadence

  • Each tax year: every rate, threshold and guide is re-checked before 6 April and the tax year label is updated.
  • Mid-year changes: if HMRC, the Scottish Government or the Student Loans Company changes a figure, affected pages are updated and re-dated.
  • Quarterly: links, sources and examples are re-read for accuracy and clarity.

Each page shows an “Updated” date. We change that date only when the page has actually been re-read.

Corrections

If you find an error, email hello@takehomelab.co.uk or see contact. Confirmed errors are fixed on the page and the date is updated.

Independence and advertising

Advertising never decides what we write or which figures we show. See the advertising disclosure and affiliate disclosure.

Take-Home Lab is informational. It is not regulated tax, pension or financial advice.
