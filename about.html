About Take-Home Lab

Take-Home Lab is an independent informational site from Rodway Labs, UK. It publishes a take-home pay calculator and short guides so you can see how income tax, National Insurance, student loan and pension contributions change a headline salary.

We built the tools. We are not an authorised tax adviser, accountant, payroll bureau or FCA-authorised firm. Figures on this site are illustrations with the assumptions written next to them.

What you will find here

  • An in-browser 2026/27 take-home calculator for England/Wales/NI or Scotland, with optional pension and one student loan plan.
  • Salary landings (for example £25k–£100k) that show a worked annual stack for that gross figure.
  • Topic guides on pay rises, bonuses, hourly pay, salary sacrifice, Scotland vs rest of UK, and student loan plans.
  • A methodology page and a glossary so the numbers are auditable.

What we will and will not do

  • We will show the method under each result and date-stamp the rates.
  • We will cite HMRC, GOV.UK and Scottish Government pages when we state a band or threshold.
  • We will not tell you which job to take, how much to put in a pension, or that a figure is “your tax bill”.
  • We will not add loan, mortgage or insurance application funnels on this site.
  • We will not treat calculator inputs as identity data: they stay in your browser for the calculation.

Rates in use

Pages on this site use the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027) unless a page says otherwise. Last checked October 2026 against HMRC employer rates and the Scottish Government bands for 2026 to 2027. When a threshold changes mid-year, we update the calculator and stamp the guide.
