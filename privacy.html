Privacy

Publisher and controller for this notice: Rodway Labs. Site: Take-Home Lab (takehomelab.co.uk).

Calculator inputs

The take-home calculator runs in your browser. Salary figures, region, pension and loan choices you type are used locally to produce the illustration. They are not sent to Rodway Labs as part of the calculation and are not stored on our servers as account data.

Advertising

Take-Home Lab can show ads via Google AdSense (publisher ID pub-7612291779397704). Google is the ad provider. When ads load, Google may process technical data (for example IP address, device and browser information, and cookie identifiers) to serve, personalise or measure ads, under Google’s policies.

On the home page, a Take-Home Lab banner asks first. It offers Essential only and Accept ads, and the AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested until you press Accept ads. Three guides (pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK) also carry an ad slot and currently load the AdSense tag with the page, where Google’s own consent tools apply. See Advertising and Cookies.

Hosting and logs

Hosting is on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical logs such as IP address, request URL, user agent and timestamps to deliver the site, cache content and protect against abuse. Those logs are processed under Cloudflare’s terms as our processor for hosting.

Consent preference

Your banner choice is stored only in your browser’s localStorage under thl-consent (essential or all). It is not a cookie and is not sent to Rodway Labs. To change it, use Reset my cookie choice on the Cookies page, or clear site data for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser; the banner will ask again on the home page.

Contact and requests

Questions about this notice: hello@takehomelab.co.uk. Because we do not run user accounts for the calculator, we typically hold little or no personal content data about visitors beyond what hosting and (on ad pages, according to your consent choice) Google process. You can also use Google’s ad settings for ad personalisation choices.
