Privacy

Updated 17 October 2026 · Tax year 2026/27

Publisher and controller for this notice: Rodway Labs. Site: Take-Home Lab (takehomelab.co.uk).

Calculator inputs

The take-home calculator runs in your browser. Salary figures, region, pension and loan choices you type are used locally to produce the illustration. They are not sent to Rodway Labs as part of the calculation and are not stored on our servers as account data.

Advertising

Take-Home Lab can show ads via Google AdSense. Google is the ad provider. When ads load, Google may process technical data (for example IP address, device and browser information, and cookie identifiers) to serve, personalise or measure ads, under Google’s policies.

Ads can appear on the home page and three guides (pay rise, salary sacrifice and Scotland vs rest of UK). On your first visit to any page, a consent dialog headed “Take-Home Lab asks for your consent to use your personal data” asks first. It explains the two purposes used (store and/or access information on a device; personalised advertising and ad measurement by Google) and offers Manage options (with Save choices and Do not consent) and Consent. The AdSense script is not loaded and no ads are requested until you choose Consent or save your choices with advertising switched on. You don’t need to consent to use the calculator, and your choices apply to this site only. Take-Home Lab runs no analytics. See Advertising and Cookies.

Contact form and emails

If you use the form on the contact page, we receive the name, email address, topic and message you enter, plus the address of the page you sent it from and the time. A Cloudflare Worker passes the message straight to our inbox by email using Cloudflare Email Routing; it is not stored in a database on the site. The inbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), which processes it as our email provider. Cloudflare uses your IP address in a rate-limiting counter that lasts about one minute, to stop repeated sending, and its standard request logs apply as described under hosting. If you email hello@takehomelab.co.uk instead, the same inbox receives it.

We use this information only to read and reply to your message, and to fix any error you report. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in answering enquiries and keeping the figures accurate. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell or share your details, or use them for advertising. We delete messages within 12 months of the conversation ending, unless we need to keep one longer to deal with a complaint or a legal claim. Please do not send payslips, tax codes, National Insurance numbers or other sensitive details. You can ask us to see or delete a message you sent by writing to hello@takehomelab.co.uk; you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Hosting and logs

Hosting is on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical logs such as IP address, request URL, user agent and timestamps to deliver the site, cache content and protect against abuse. Those logs are processed under Cloudflare’s terms as our processor for hosting.

Consent preference

Your choice is stored only in your browser’s localStorage under thl-consent, as a small record of the notice version (v: 2), your choice (essential or all) and the time you chose. It applies to this site only. Choices made with the earlier banner are not carried over, so everyone is asked once more. It is not a cookie and is not sent to Rodway Labs. To change it, use the Privacy preferences link at the bottom of any page, which reopens the dialog, or clear site data for takehomelab.co.uk in your browser; the dialog will then ask again on the next page you open. After you accept, Google may set cookies such as __gads, __gpi, __eoi and IDE; see Cookies.

Contact and requests

Questions about this notice: hello@takehomelab.co.uk. Because we do not run user accounts for the calculator, we typically hold little or no personal data about visitors beyond messages you send us, what hosting processes and (on ad pages, according to your consent choice) what Google processes. You can also use Google’s ad settings for ad personalisation choices.
