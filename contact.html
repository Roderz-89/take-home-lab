Report a figure error

If a guide or the calculator gives a number you think is wrong, tell us. It helps to include:

  • the page address (for example /guides/take-home-40000);
  • the inputs you used: salary, region (Scotland or the rest of the UK), pension type and percentage, student loan plan;
  • the figure you expected and where it came from (a payslip line, HMRC guidance or another calculator).

We check every report against the published 2026/27 HMRC and Scottish Government rates and correct confirmed errors on the page, with a new “Updated” date. The method we check against is on the methodology page.

What we cannot help with

We do not give personal tax, pension or financial advice by email, and we cannot see your payslip, tax code or HMRC record. If your question is “what should I do with this job offer?”, that is for you, a regulated adviser or HMRC. For your own tax code or a refund, use your HMRC personal tax account.

Privacy

Emails are used only to reply to you and are not added to a mailing list. The calculator runs in your browser and sends nothing to us. See the privacy notice.
